## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality
The brand embodies an uncompromising synthesis of motorsport precision and institutional road safety. Operating out of Melbourne, it bridges the adrenaline-fueled aspiration of young learners stepping behind the wheel for the first time with the unyielding demand for vigilance, reliability, and calm required by parents and regulatory standards (VicRoads). The tone is confident, structured, dynamic, and protective—never dull, yet never reckless.

### Emotional Response
The interface must instill:
- **Authority & Reassurance:** Rigorous testing standards, defensive driving techniques, and top-tier instructor accreditation.
- **Empowerment & High Performance:** The excitement of automotive autonomy, mastery of vehicle control, and modern car culture.
- **Clarity & Urgency:** Frictionless test package bookings, transparent pricing, and instant instructor coordination.

### Design Movement: High-Octane Athleticism × Modern Precision
The visual language fuses **High-Contrast Bold** automotive performance with **Refined Glassmorphism** and tactical instrumentation.
- **Motorsport-Inspired Visual Geometry:** Shield contours, angled badge cutaways, and structured telemetry dashboards.
- **Aero Carbon & Surface Layers:** Deep tarmac charcoals paired with pristine technical whites and brushed graphite cards.
- **Plate Semiotics:** Direct functional integration of Victoria's iconic licensing milestones—Learner Amber (`#F59E0B`), Red P-Plate (`#E5242A`), and Green P-Plate (`#0F8A52`) serving as clear categorical and progression indicators.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the school's motorsport shield emblem and the official Victorian licensing journey (L-plate yellow, Red probationary, Green probationary).

### Color Logic & Application
- **Primary (`#E5242A` - Apex Red):** Drives high-conversion focal points, primary action triggers, test readiness calls-to-action, urgent status indicators, and the prominent red P-plate journey marker.
- **Secondary (`#0F8A52` - VicRoads Emerald):** Applied to safety certifications, verified instructor accreditations, defensive driving milestones, success states, and the second-stage green P-plate tier.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Learner Amber):** Dedicated to novice milestone highlights, advisory flags, in-progress hours tracking, and Learner plate badging.
- **Neutral Core (`#111315` - Asphalt Noir & `#1E2228` - Graphite Chassis):** Anchors the application with deep, cinematic track surfaces. Pure black is avoided in favor of rich, metallic carbon tones that support high contrast with crisp white text (`#F8FAFC`) and muted chrome dividers (`#333A44`).
- **Surface Elevation Contours:** Higher elevations step smoothly from `#111315` (canvas) to `#1A1D22` (surface-base), `#22272F` (surface-elevated), and `#2D333E` (surface-highlight).

## Typography

The type system blends aerodynamic velocity with surgical instrumentation legibility.

- **Headlines (Chivo):** An aggressive, performance-geared neo-grotesque with sharp terminals and solid structural geometry. Set in heavy weights (700 to 900) to echo automotive badging, speedometers, and protective heraldic shields. Displays and section banners leverage slight uppercase styling with tracked letter-spacing (+0.04em) to project authority.
- **Body & Instrumentation (Space Grotesk):** Provides a crisp, technical aesthetic suited for driver logbooks, package terms, VicRoads regulations, and test pass criteria. It preserves high readability at low contrast ratios on dark surfaces.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Kept uppercase in weights 600–700 with letter-spacing (+0.06em) for telemetry badges, transmission options (Auto/Manual), instructor rating badges, and licensing tier indicators (L, P1, P2).

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` (32px) outer canvas margin, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Maximum content constraint: `1280px` for optimal telemetry dashboards, package comparison tables, and interactive booking funnels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` margin and `1.25rem` gutters. Driver schedule cards collapse into a 2-column format.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margin and `1rem` gutters. Vertical stacked flow with sticky bottom-anchored booking action bar (`0470 452 803` direct hotline & "Book Drive").

### Spacing Rhythm
Built on an industrial 4px/8px incremental scale:
- `space-xs` (4px): Metric pill padding, badge internal gaps.
- `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text gaps, form input vertical padding.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard card inner padding, sub-element separation.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card-to-card stack distance, instructor bio layout flow.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section subgroup gaps, feature grid row spacing.
- `space-2xl` (64px): Hero block padding, major transition separators between package offerings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an automotive dashboard hierarchy: dark tonal layering, razor-sharp perimeter chamfers, and glowing ambient kinetic lights.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Track Canvas):** Flat `#111315`. Background patterns use 45-degree micro-angled racing hatchings or subtle carbon-weave opacity (4%) to prevent digital sterility.
2. **Level 1 (Cockpit Panels / Cards):** `#1E2228` with a 1px border stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hovered & Active Nodes):** `#252A32` elevated by dynamic ambient color glows:
   - Apex Booking Nodes: `0 12px 32px rgba(229, 36, 42, 0.22)` with 1px border `rgba(229, 36, 42, 0.4)`.
   - Verified Safety Modules: `0 12px 32px rgba(15, 138, 82, 0.22)` with 1px border `rgba(15, 138, 82, 0.4)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Sticky Action Bar):** Glassmorphic composite using `#181B20` at 88% opacity with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and top-edge highlight (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).

### Edges & Structural Borders
No blunt outlines without direction. Card borders use gradient directional strokes fading from light metallic grey down to transparent, mimicking directional automotive body seams.

## Shapes

### Chamfers & Shield Language
The design system adopts a soft-geometric (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base radius) structure with selective shield cutoffs and technical angled corners. Rather than bubbly or overly-organic curves, shapes reflect vehicle coachwork, aerodynamic winglets, and defensive shields.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges):** 4px (`rounded-sm`).
- **Standard Surfaces (Cards, Lesson Tiles):** 8px (`rounded-md`).
- **Milestone Badges (L, P1, P2 Identifiers):** Exact square ratios with 6px roundedness, mirroring actual Australian vehicle exterior plates with heavy 2px inset borders.
- **Shield Accents & Feature Cards:** Top-left and bottom-right subtle corner bevels (45-degree chamfers) applied to premium package modules to reflect the Safe-Gen shield crest.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Book Driving Lesson / Test"):** Solid Apex Red (`#E5242A`) fill with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text in `Chivo` Bold uppercase. Sharp 4px radius with a subtle top bevel highlight. Hover state activates a red ambient spotlight glow (`rgba(229, 36, 42, 0.4)`) and `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary ("Instructor Accreditation / VicRoads Info"):** Deep Graphite (`#22272F`) surface with a 1px border in Emerald Racing Green (`#0F8A52`) and `#E2E8F0` text. Hover transitions background to `#0F8A52` with crisp white text.
- **Phone Action Trigger:** Distinct pill badge echoing the logo banner (`0470 452 803`) featuring a circular red icon badge juxtaposed against an asphalt dark pill casing.

### Chips & Licensing Badges
- **Milestone Plates:**
  - **L-Plate:** High-vis yellow background (`#F59E0B`), bold black "L" glyph, thick black stroke.
  - **Red P-Plate:** Apex red background (`#E5242A`), pure white "P" glyph, thick black stroke.
  - **Green P-Plate:** Emerald green background (`#0F8A52`), pure white "P" glyph, thick black stroke.
- **Transmission Tags:** Micro-badges indicating `AUTO` or `MANUAL` with mono-spaced styling, 1px border, and dark charcoal backdrop.

### Cards
- **Lesson Package Cards:** Dark graphite canvas (`#1E2228`) topped with an integrated status bar (indicating hour count, e.g., "5-Lesson Value Pack" or "VicRoads Drive Test Prep"). Elevated headers accommodate dynamic pricing badges paired with key checklist indicators (e.g., dual controls, 5-star ANCAP i30 fleet).

### Input Fields
- Dark slate fill (`#15181C`), 1px structural border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), 4px radius. Focus state swaps border to Apex Red (`#E5242A`) with a subtle 2px blurred focus halo. Floating labels in `Space Grotesk` uppercase micro-type (`11px`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px boxes with 2px corner radius. Unchecked: `#22272F` with border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked: Apex Red fill with clean white chevron tick. Radio buttons use a concentric ring model with red active core.

### Telemetry & Progress Meters (VicRoads Logbook Tracker)
- Custom progress component displaying daytime (0–100 hrs) and nighttime (0–20 hrs) mandatory driving requirements. Filled track features an emerald gradient with milestone hash marks for easy supervision by both student and supervising parent.
