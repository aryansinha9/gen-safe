About your instructor

Hi, I’m Zubair.

With over 25 years of driving experience, I bring extensive knowledge, practical experience and a genuine passion for helping people become safe and confident drivers.

Throughout my journey, I’ve had the privilege of training students and family members across different countries, including the Middle East, America, India and now Australia. Those experiences have taught me to adapt to different driving environments, road conditions and learning needs.

For the past one and a half years I’ve been training students here in Australia, helping learners develop the skills, confidence and road awareness they need to become responsible drivers.

I believe learning to drive should be a calm, positive and supportive experience. I teach with patience and make every lesson comfortable and easy to understand.

“My goal isn’t simply to help you pass your driving test. It’s to help you build safe driving habits and confidence that will last a lifetime.”
