Terms of Enrolment

  • Lessons are 1-on-1 and include pickup and drop-off within our service zones.
  • Learners must carry a valid Victorian learner permit (or eligible overseas/interstate licence) to every lesson. We can’t start a lesson without it.
  • Packages are valid for 12 months from purchase and may be transferred to an immediate family member.
  • Payment is due before or at the first lesson of a package. Prices are in AUD and include GST.
  • Instructors may end a lesson early if a student is affected by alcohol, drugs or fatigue, or if continuing would be unsafe. The lesson is charged in full.
  • Test day car hire covers use of our vehicle and instructor. The VicRoads test fee is paid by the student directly to VicRoads.

Cancellation Policy

  • 24 hours’ notice or more: free cancellation or reschedule.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: 50% of the lesson fee is charged (or half a lesson deducted from a package).
  • No-show: full lesson fee is charged.
  • If we cancel — for instructor illness, vehicle issues or unsafe weather — you’ll be rebooked at no cost.
  • Test day hire cancelled by VicRoads is rescheduled free. Cancelled by the student within 48 hours: $100 fee.

To cancel or reschedule, call or text 0470 452 803.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to run your lessons: your name, contact details, pickup address, licence stage and lesson notes. We use this to schedule lessons, contact you about bookings and track your progress.

  • We never sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.
  • Progress updates are shared with a parent or supervisor only when you (or, for under-18s, your parent/guardian) ask us to.
  • You can request access to, or deletion of, your information at any time by contacting us.
  • This website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

VicRoads Compliance

  • All instructors hold a current Driving Instructor Authority issued in Victoria and a Certificate IV in Motor Vehicle Driver Training.
  • All instructors hold a current Working with Children Check and national police check.
  • Our training vehicles are fitted with dual controls, display L-plates during lessons, and are comprehensively insured for driver training.
  • Each professional lesson hour is recorded in your learner logbook. Logbook credit rules are set by VicRoads — see the VicRoads learner logbook page for current requirements.
