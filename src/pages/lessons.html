How Close Are You to 120 Hours?

Learners under 21 need 120 supervised hours, including 20 at night, before sitting the drive test. Each hour with a professional instructor counts as three logbook hours — for up to 10 lessons (30 hours).

Always confirm current requirements with VicRoads — rules can change.

Pricing FAQ

Are there extra fees for weekends or pickup?add

No. Prices are the same seven days a week and include pickup and drop-off anywhere in our service zones.

How long are packages valid?add

Packages are valid for 12 months from purchase. Unused lessons can be transferred to a family member.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?add

Free with 24 hours’ notice. See our full cancellation policy.

Do I pay the VicRoads test fee to you?add

No — you book and pay for the drive test directly with VicRoads. Our test day package covers the car, warm-up lesson and instructor.

Can I use a Keys2Drive free lesson?add

Yes. Our instructors are Keys2Drive accredited — mention it when you book and bring your supervising driver along.
