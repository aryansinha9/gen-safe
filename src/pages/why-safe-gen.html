The Safe-Gen Method

A Four-Phase Curriculum

Every learner follows the same proven progression — paced to you. Your instructor records skills after each lesson so you (and your parents) always know what’s next.

  1. LPhase 01

    Foundations

    Cockpit drill, steering, pedal feel and observation in quiet streets. The dual brake means zero-pressure first drives.

  2. LPhase 02

    Traffic Craft

    Intersections, lane changes, roundabouts, trams and hook turns. Hazard perception drills built into every drive.

  3. PPhase 03

    Test Readiness

    Mock tests scored like the real thing, on roads around your chosen test centre. We fix critical errors before test day.

  4. PPhase 04

    Solo Confidence

    Freeway merging, night and wet-weather driving, and defensive habits that keep new P-platers safe on their own.
